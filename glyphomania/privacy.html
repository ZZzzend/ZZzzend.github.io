Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Glyphomania.

Data collection and tracking

Glyphomania does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data through the app. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, user accounts, or third-party SDKs that collect user data.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, completed levels, stars, unlocked symbols, challenge progress, and sound and haptic preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game functionality and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Paths and symbols you draw are processed on your device during gameplay. They are not uploaded or retained after they are used by the game.

Data retention and deletion

You can use Settings > Reset Progress to delete completed levels, stars, unlocked symbols, and challenge progress. Sound and haptic preferences are kept when progress is reset. Deleting the app removes all locally stored app data.

Support communications

If you contact support by email, the developer receives the email address, message, and any diagnostic details you choose to provide. This information is used only to respond to your request and is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for support. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting the address below.

External pages

The app can open its support and privacy pages only when you select the corresponding link. Your browser and the website hosting provider may process standard connection information under their own privacy terms. Glyphomania does not receive this information through the app.

Children's privacy

Glyphomania does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app or legal requirements. The latest version will always be available on this page, with the revision date shown above.
