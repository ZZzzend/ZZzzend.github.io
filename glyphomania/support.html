Glyphomania Support

Need help, found a problem, or want to share feedback? Contact us and we will usually respond within a few days.

Email Support

Before contacting support

  • Make sure you are using the latest available version of Glyphomania.
  • Close and reopen the app. If the issue remains, restart your device.
  • Check the in-game Settings if sound or haptic feedback is unavailable.

Reporting a problem

Please include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Glyphomania version, level or challenge round, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are helpful when available.

Please do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information.

Progress and settings

Progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Glyphomania does not currently use accounts or cloud synchronization, so deleting the app also deletes its local progress.

Reset Progress in Settings removes completed levels, stars, unlocked symbols, and challenge progress. Sound and haptic preferences are kept.

Privacy

Glyphomania does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking. Read the complete Privacy Policy.
